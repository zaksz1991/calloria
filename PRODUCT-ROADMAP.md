# CALLORIA Product Roadmap

## v1.1.0 — Customer Intelligence & Adaptive Contact Simulation
Completed in this package: synthetic persona engine, scenario generation, adaptive turns, deterministic practice QA, critical breach handling and history metrics.

## Next priorities
1. v1.3.0 — Organisation-configured AI provider adapter: optional model-assisted transcript analysis with explicit tenant consent, provider keys stored server-side, data minimisation, redaction, cost controls and human approval.
2. v1.4.0 — Realtime operations: event-level persistence, optimistic concurrency, queue presence and supervisor monitoring.
3. v1.5.0 — QA recording pipeline: consented audio attachment, secure storage, retention rules, playback permissions and redacted transcript alignment.
4. Production readiness: live Supabase migration tests, tenant-isolation tests, build pipeline, accessibility and physical mobile/browser validation.

## v1.2.0 — Human QA & Supervisor Intelligence
Completed in this package: weight-configurable scorecard versions, transcript-evidence-linked human reviews, coaching actions, reviewer calibration, dispute submission and resolution, and QA reporting. The reviewer workflow is not yet protected by shared server-side supervisor permissions.

## Release boundary
The current QA engine is deterministic and local. Do not describe it as AI-powered or as a certified quality scorecard.
