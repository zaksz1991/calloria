# CALLORIA Product Roadmap

## v1.1.0 — Customer Intelligence & Adaptive Contact Simulation
Completed in this package: synthetic persona engine, scenario generation, adaptive turns, deterministic practice QA, critical breach handling and history metrics.

## Next priorities
1. v1.2.0 — Human QA workspace: configurable scorecards, call review, evidence-linked coaching, supervisor calibration and dispute workflow.
2. v1.3.0 — Organisation-configured AI provider adapter: optional model-assisted transcript analysis with explicit tenant consent, provider keys stored server-side, data minimisation, redaction, cost controls and human approval.
3. v1.4.0 — Realtime operations: event-level persistence, optimistic concurrency, queue presence and supervisor monitoring.
4. Production readiness: live Supabase migration tests, tenant-isolation tests, build pipeline, accessibility and physical mobile/browser validation.

## Release boundary
The current QA engine is deterministic and local. Do not describe it as AI-powered or as a certified quality scorecard.
