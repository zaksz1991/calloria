# CALLORIA v6.67 — Verification report

**Release:** Campaign Contact Worklists & Dialler Retry Governance  
**Source:** user-supplied cumulative `CALLORIA-v6.66-LIVE-OUTBOUND-OPERATIONS.zip`  
**Preparation runtime:** Node.js v22.16.0

## Delivered in source

- Added `js/campaign-operations-engine.js`, a deterministic worklist and outbound-attempt lifecycle engine.
- Created eight synthetic contact records for each campaign, preserving each campaign's original template customer as contact one.
- Added stable campaign-contact IDs, bounded attempt history, attempt owner/reference, current status, last outcome and next permitted attempt time.
- Added deterministic dial outcomes: answered, no answer, busy and voicemail.
- Added distinct retry cooling-off times: 15 minutes for no answer, 30 minutes for busy and 120 minutes for voicemail.
- Enforced a three-attempt default ceiling; contacts that reach the ceiling without connection become exhausted.
- Added customer-requested callbacks that retain the original campaign contact ID and require a valid future due time. Due callbacks resume through the callback control and eligibility check.
- Persisted completed, declined, opt-out/suppressed, review-required and exhausted states.
- Added campaign worklist metrics for eligible contacts, attempts, callback due, retries waiting, completed, suppressed, review and exhausted contacts.
- Integrated contact-level worklists into `js/main.js`, retaining the v6.66 live outbound conversation, CRM and after-contact workflow for answered contacts.
- Added responsive campaign-contact rows and dialler outcome panel styles.
- Added engine and UI integration tests; extended `npm run check` to syntax-check the new engine and updated the package-manifest regression test to match.

## Product and safety boundaries

- All campaign worklist contacts, identities and dial outcomes are fictional local game records. No real phone number, email address or external customer information is generated by this engine.
- A deterministic `answered` result enters the existing v6.66 live outbound customer conversation. No real telephone call is placed.
- No PBX, live dialler, banking system, external campaign provider or third-party contact database is connected.
- The retry/callback timers are gameplay rules, not approved legal or compliance policy for real campaigns. Any real deployment would need organisation-approved consent, suppression, lawful contact windows, attempt limits and retention rules.
- Recruitment and assessment remain the employment entry gate. The main product remains the employed-agent contact-centre operations and career simulator, not a training-only quiz.
- The source archive did not contain `public/assets/` or the user's GLB binaries. Those assets are not modified or replaced by this release.
