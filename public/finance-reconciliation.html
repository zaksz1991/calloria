CONTROL PRINCIPLES

Matching and period-close rules

Automatic matching: exact bank reference, receipt reference, amount and currency must agree. Amount-only matches are suggestions, never automatic.

Manual matching: amount and currency must still agree. The operator must record a reason and identity.

Other movements: bank fees, transfers, interest and other items need a category, evidence and responsible person.

Period close: every bank line and Billing v5.8 receipt in the period must be accounted for. Approval requires a second person. Approved periods are locked in this browser workspace.
