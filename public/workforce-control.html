Forecast methodology

Required agents = rounded-up total handling workload ÷ (30-minute interval × occupancy target). This capacity proxy does not model arrival distribution, service-level targets, shrinkage, concurrency or queue abandonment. Use a validated WFM model before making real staffing decisions.
