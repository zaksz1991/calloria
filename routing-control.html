ROUTING RULES

Eligibility and capacity

Skill match
Required skill, department, channel and customer language must all match your approved access map.

Workforce presence
You must be clocked in and on a customer-work activity block in the Workforce Control Centre.

Concurrency
Active, held and after-contact work items count against your configured capacity.

Fairness
Higher priority work is served first. Long-waiting work receives priority aging so it does not remain unseen indefinitely.
