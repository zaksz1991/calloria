# CALLORIA v5.6 — Voice of Customer & Experience Intelligence

An additive public-page module for capturing customer experience feedback, calculating CSAT/NPS/CES, and managing recovery work generated by negative service signals.

## Included

- Customer feedback register with search and filters for promoters, passives, detractors and recovery-triggered responses.
- CSAT satisfaction score (1–5), NPS recommendation score (0–10) and customer effort score (1–5, where 5 means very easy).
- Dashboard metrics: average CSAT, satisfied percentage, NPS, average ease score, open recovery tasks and overdue commitments.
- NPS distribution, feedback theme distribution and CSAT by contact channel.
- Import of completed interactions from v5.5 `calloria.agent-workbench.v55` as local feedback-entry candidates.
- Manual response capture linked to imported contact, customer and case references.
- Automatic service-recovery task creation when CSAT is 1–2, NPS is 0–6, or CES is 1–2.
- 24-hour recovery target, owner assignment, task status, update history and mandatory documented outcome for resolution/closure.
- Explicit one-click attachment of captured scores to the matching v5.4 Customer 360 case, including a satisfaction history item and case timeline event.
- CSV exports for feedback and recovery registers.
- Responsive desktop and mobile layout.

## Installation

This is an overlay patch, not a full CALLORIA repository export. Copy these four files from the package's `public/` folder into the repository's existing `public/` folder:

- `voice-of-customer.html`
- `voice-of-customer.css`
- `voice-of-customer.js`
- `voice-of-customer-engine.js`

Do not remove or overwrite the v5.4 Customer Journey or v5.5 Agent Workbench files. The new page is standalone but uses their browser-local storage keys for optional read/write-through integration.

After the files are committed and deployed, open:

`https://calloria.vercel.app/voice-of-customer.html`

## Use

1. Review the clearly marked sample feedback records and dashboard.
2. Use **Import completed contacts** to read completed contact interactions stored by the v5.5 workbench in the same browser and origin.
3. Select **Record response** for an imported candidate, or use **Record feedback** for an unlinked manual response.
4. Record CSAT, NPS, ease of service, primary theme and the customer's comment.
5. Low satisfaction, detractor NPS or difficult effort automatically opens a recovery task with a 24-hour target.
6. Assign a named owner and move the recovery task through its status lifecycle. A documented outcome is required before resolving or closing it.
7. For a linked v5.4 case, use **Attach scores to Customer 360 case** to explicitly write the response into that case's satisfaction field, satisfaction history and timeline.
8. Export feedback and recovery registers separately as CSV.

## Metric definitions

- **CSAT average:** arithmetic mean of the 1–5 satisfaction scores.
- **Satisfied percentage:** percentage of responses rated 4 or 5.
- **NPS:** percentage of promoters (9–10) minus percentage of detractors (0–6); passives (7–8) are neutral.
- **CES:** arithmetic mean of the 1–5 ease score. A score of 5 means very easy; scores 1–2 trigger follow-up.

All metrics are descriptive of the currently stored response sample. They are not statistically weighted or adjusted for survey response bias.

## Data and integration boundaries

- Feedback data is stored under `calloria.voice-of-customer.v56` in browser local storage.
- v5.5 import reads completed contact records only. It creates local response-entry candidates; it does not contact customers or dispatch surveys.
- v5.4 integration is explicit and writes to `calloria.customer-journey.v54` only after the operator selects the case-sync action.
- There is no external survey channel, email/SMS delivery, telephony integration, shared multi-agent database, central analytics warehouse or live product telemetry.
- The dashboard includes synthetic sample records on first launch, visibly marked as sample data. Do not treat sample metrics as production results.
- Storage is local to one browser profile and origin. Clearing browser storage removes the locally stored demonstration data.
- Do not enter real confidential customer, payment, authentication, health or other sensitive personal information.
