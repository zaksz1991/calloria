# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — Professional 3D workplace vertical slice

- Replaced the earlier low-detail room study with a purpose-built contact-centre floor.
- Added 12 detailed workstations with dual monitors, simulated CRM/queue screens, phone, headset, keyboard, mouse, chair, drawers, acoustic dividers and station indicators.
- Added reception, supervisor/QA suite, meeting area, operations wall display, glazing, ceiling structure, lights, materials and surface textures.
- Added runtime GLTF loading for two CC0 rigged employee models and a CC0 reception desk asset, with local fallback geometry.
- Added first-person movement, mouse look, preview orbit/zoom and workstation proximity interactions.
- Preserved the recruitment gate, existing inbound/outbound scenario experiences, CRM, knowledge base, performance and career views.
- Added an asset register, technical limits, roadmap and static regression tests.

## 0.1.0 — Initial functional prototype

- Established recruitment application and assessment flow.
- Added employment offer, initial inbound/outbound call scenarios, CRM, knowledge base, performance and career foundations.
- Added the first low-detail contact-centre scene study.
