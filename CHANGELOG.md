# CALLORIA v4.1.0 — Interactive On-the-Job Shadowing

- Replaces the old five-step read-and-click shadowing carousel with one continuous, stateful training interaction.
- Demonstrates queue offer acceptance, agent greeting/listening, CRM logging, approved knowledge-base review and after-contact disposition.
- Enforces the workflow order and validates CRM notes, knowledge decision and wrap-up disposition.
- Persists the training session locally in the existing employment profile.
- Preserves the general customer experience default and all specialist industry packs.
- Keeps training records separate from production calls handled, QA, CSAT, salary and live customer cases.
- Keeps trainer sign-off and desk allocation gated on all four OJT modules.
