# Changelog — CALLORIA v5.5

## Added
- Unified frontline queue for six inbound contact channels.
- Agent shift and presence controls with one-active-contact enforcement.
- Contact transcript, simulated agent replies with deterministic customer follow-up turns, handling cues, internal notes and specialist transfer.
- Customer 360 and open-case context from v5.4.
- Published knowledge article search and case-reference attachment from v5.2.
- Published service notice and open incident awareness from v5.2/v5.3.
- Mandatory wrap-up disposition and summary.
- Customer Journey write-through: create a case, append to an open case, or record a customer note.
- Contact metrics, completed-contact list and safe CSV export.
- Responsive desktop/mobile layout.

## Data boundary
- Synthetic queue and transcript records are stored in browser local storage.
- No live channels, telephony, shared queue, central database, automatic service notifications or AI endpoint are connected.
