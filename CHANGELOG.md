# Changelog — v4.3.0

- Replaced the one-click OJT sign-off with a separate trainer evidence review, approval and workstation allocation sequence.
- Added four trainer attestations: training evidence reviewed, customer-care readiness, safe conduct and simulated system proficiency.
- Added ten operating departments and twelve selectable workstation positions.
- Required all departments to retain universal customer-care responsibility in addition to specialist skills.
- Made the first independent shift require workstation allocation and attendance at the relevant morning or afternoon operations briefing.
- Recorded the first independent shift only after the existing shift engine confirms that a shift is active.
- Defaulted new employee OJT to general customer experience rather than assuming a bank contact-centre role.
- Migrated completed v4.2 trainees without production work to workstation allocation, while preserving existing employees with prior work.
- Preserved v4.1 shadowing and v4.2 supervised customer practice, including training-only evidence boundaries.
