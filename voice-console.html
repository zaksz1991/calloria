Caller numbers remain masked. Call recording and transcription are disabled in this release. Live hold, transfer, conference and browser microphone controls are not enabled.

Operational boundaries

Inbound routing uses a single designated on-call handset per department, configured by an administrator. CALLORIA records call status and links the interaction to skills-based routing; it does not yet provide a browser softphone or dynamically select an available agent from the live workforce roster. Agents answer on their configured phone. Do not record calls outside a separately approved consent and retention implementation.
