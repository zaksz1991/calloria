# CALLORIA v3.9.0 — Staff GLB asset provenance

The selected character models are listed individually in Gobkit's published free-model manifest at https://gobkit.com/api/free. The manifest identifies the two source packs as CC0 1.0 public-domain content for commercial or personal use, and lists rigged low-poly GLB models with named `Idle`, `Walk` and `Run` animation clips.

## License record

The source manifest's model and pack metadata state: `CC0 1.0 (public domain) — free for any use, commercial or personal, no attribution required.` CALLORIA records the declared source and licence alongside each downloaded file's SHA-256 hash in `calloria-cc0-staff-manifest.json`.

The actual binary downloads and visual suitability must still be checked by the deployment/build and browser acceptance tests. This provenance record establishes the source metadata, not the visual quality of the asset or a runtime rendering result.
